# RSI Source Review: November 28, 2026

**Search and review date:** 2026-11-28, Asia/Shanghai. **Baseline:** `5b68116`. This was a bounded screening pass of November 25–28 and previously queued work, not exhaustive retrieval of all new submissions. Both additions first appeared on November 25 UTC and are reviewed here at v1.

## Admitted reviews

- [TransHarness](../../papers/2026/transharness.md) studies frozen coding-harness transfer using archived success–failure contrasts, executable triggers and semantic review. Its reported improvements are positive point estimates with paired intervals including zero; thinner archives and premature interventions can regress.
- [EvoLink-Bench](../../papers/2026/evolink-bench.md) compares state reset, state retention without explicit updates, and state retention with updates where supported. Knowledge and execution scores can move differently. The historical panels do not isolate a causal update effect and the public runtime is not a complete paper reproduction.

The catalog rises from 42 to 44 reviews. Readers should compare how many previously successful executions are disrupted, not just how many failures are rescued. For persistent agents, retain a frozen-state control and preserve both task artifacts and learner-state provenance. No better-improver claim or independent experimental result was added.
