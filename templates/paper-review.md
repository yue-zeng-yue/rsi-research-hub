# Paper Review Template

Use this structure for a full review. Replace instructional text with source-grounded content. Retain `Not verified` only when accompanied by a precise evidence gap. State the review date and whether results are author-reported or independently reproduced.

## Full Paper Title

Record the exact title of the inspected version. Explain renamed revisions or ambiguous acronyms.

## Authors and Affiliations

List authors in source order and map affiliations from the title page. Do not infer employment from a code-hosting organization or an older paper.

## Publication Date

Separate the first public submission, reviewed revision, and conference publication date. Record the cutoff used for inclusion.

## Venue or Preprint Status

Use an official proceedings or venue record for acceptance claims. Label author-reported status separately. Distinguish workshops from main-conference proceedings.

## Paper URL / DOI

Link the canonical record and exact reviewed full-text version. Include a DOI only if its source identity is verified; state pending registration where indicated.

## Official GitHub Repository

Provide the author-linked repository, relevant branch or commit, and evidence of association. Distinguish announcement, partial release, and substantive implementation.

## Research Problem

Specify the target limitation, optimization object, feedback available, and intended distribution of use.

## Methodology and Algorithm

Explain state, inputs, candidate generation, update rule, acceptance, memory, and stopping conditions. Include necessary equations and identify fixed versus editable components. Cite method sections.

## Key Contributions

Separate the authors' claimed novelty from this review's assessment of overlap with prior work.

## Experimental Setup

Record executor, proposer, teacher, and evaluator identities; task splits; budgets; search iterations; seeds; training settings; and version constraints.

## Benchmarks and Baselines

Name benchmark releases and subsets. Identify baseline implementations, inherited scores, adaptation differences, and feedback/resource matching.

## Quantitative Results

For each number, give metric, comparator, model, benchmark, conditions, uncertainty, and source table/figure. Distinguish absolute and relative changes and selected peaks from final artifacts. Leave an unverifiable number out of the conclusion.

## Limitations

Separate author-acknowledged limitations from review analysis. Discuss generalization, contamination, stochastic selection, evaluator coupling, cost, and mechanism attribution where relevant.

## Reproducibility

Record available code, data, configurations, artifacts, and access gaps. State exactly what was inspected or executed. Code availability alone is not a successful reproduction.

## Open Research Questions

Propose concrete unresolved questions, nearest prior work, and experiments that could falsify the extension. Do not present proposed improvements as findings.
